# 30 dream journal prompts

For mornings when you remember only a little. Pick one and write for two minutes.

## When the dream is fading

1. What is the very last image I remember before waking?
2. What feeling was I left with, even if I can't say why?
3. Was I inside, outside, or somewhere that was both?
4. Was I alone? If not, who or what was there?
5. What was I trying to do?
6. Was there a colour, sound or texture that stood out?
7. If the dream were a film, what would its title be?

## Going deeper into the scene

8. Where did the dream begin, and how did the place change?
9. What was the most surprising moment?
10. What did I do that I would not do awake?
11. Did anyone speak? What do I remember of the words?
12. What was in the background that I almost didn't notice?
13. Was I watching myself or seeing through my own eyes?
14. How did time move: fast, slow, jumping?

## Feelings

15. What was the strongest emotion, and when did it peak?
16. Did my feeling in the dream match what was happening?
17. How did the feeling change when I woke up?
18. Where in my body do I notice the dream now?

## Personal associations

19. Does a place in the dream remind me of somewhere real?
20. Does a person in the dream remind me of someone, even if they looked different?
21. What does the main image mean to me personally, not in a dictionary?
22. Have I seen this image or theme in an earlier dream?
23. Did anything from yesterday appear in a changed form?
24. Is there something I have been avoiding thinking about that this touches?

## Looking across dreams

25. Which themes have I tagged most often this month?
26. Do certain feelings cluster on certain days or after certain events?
27. Which dream this month do I still think about, and why?
28. What has changed in my recurring dream since the first time?

## Closing the entry

29. What is one detail I want to keep from this dream?
30. If I could ask the dream one question, what would it be?
