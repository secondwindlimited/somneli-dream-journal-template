## 3. Reflect

- In the dreams where this theme shows up, what was I feeling?
- Is there a detail or personal association that links these entries?
- Even when the theme repeats, what changes from one dream to the next?
- Was anything similar going on in my life around each date?

## 4. One sentence to keep

>

A repeated word is a starting point, not a conclusion. This worksheet does not diagnose anything. If dreams or sleep are distressing you, talk to a qualified professional.
