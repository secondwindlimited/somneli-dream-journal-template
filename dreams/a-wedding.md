# What does it mean to dream about a wedding?

A wedding dream can bring up commitment, change, belonging, or pressure, but no image has one fixed meaning. It is not a prediction of marriage or a reliable clue about another person. Your circumstances and the dream’s details may offer more useful starting points.

## What are dreams about a wedding often connected with?

- Commitment or choice
- A life transition
- Social expectations
- Connection and belonging

## What should you ask yourself?

- What commitment or choice is taking up attention in my life?
- Where do I feel ready for change, and where do I feel unsure?
- Whose expectations did I notice in the dream, and how do they compare with my own?
- Who was present, and how did I feel around them?
- What detail of the dream stayed with me after waking, and why might it matter to me?

## How do you write this dream down?

If this dream appears again, write down who was getting married, where the ceremony took place, and what changed or went wrong. Record your feelings before and after waking, then note any current decisions, transitions, or social pressures that come to mind.
