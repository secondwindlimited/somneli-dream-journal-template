# What does it mean to dream about fire?

Fire has no fixed meaning in a dream. People often connect it with intense feelings, change, conflict, energy, or something that feels out of control. Your waking circumstances and the fire’s behavior may offer more useful clues than a general symbol list.

## What are dreams about fire often connected with?

- Strong emotion
- Change and endings
- Energy and creativity
- Safety and control

## What should you ask yourself?

- What in my life feels intense or difficult to contain right now?
- What was the fire affecting, and what does that object or place mean to me?
- Did I feel afraid, calm, energized, or responsible in the dream?
- Am I facing a transition or trying to let go of something?
- What support or space would help me feel more secure?

## How do you write this dream down?

Next time this dream appears, write down where the fire started, how it behaved, what it touched, and who was present. Record your feelings during and after the dream, then note any waking-life pressures or changes that come to mind.
