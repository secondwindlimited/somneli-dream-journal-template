# What does it mean to dream about brakes not working?

A dream about brakes that fail is often associated with feeling unable to slow down, regain control, or pause before a decision. It is not a prediction or warning about an accident. The setting, your response, and current pressures may offer more personal context.

## What are dreams about brakes not working often connected with?

- Loss of control
- Pressure to keep going
- A difficult decision
- Dependence on support

## What should you ask yourself?

- Where in my life do I feel unable to slow things down?
- What responsibility or change currently feels hard to manage?
- What was I trying to stop or protect in the dream?
- How did I respond when the brakes failed, and does that resemble my usual response?
- What kind of pause or support would feel useful right now?

## How do you write this dream down?

Next time this dream appears, write down where you were driving, who was with you, and what happened when you pressed the brakes. Record your strongest feeling and any waking situation that brings a similar sense of speed or pressure.
