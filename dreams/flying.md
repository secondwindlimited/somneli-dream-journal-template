# What does it mean to dream about flying?

Flying dreams are often associated with freedom, ambition, escape, or a wish for more control. They can also reflect pressure or uncertainty, depending on how the flight feels. These are starting points, not universal meanings; your circumstances and the dream’s details matter.

## What are dreams about flying often connected with?

- Freedom and choice
- A wider perspective
- Getting away
- Control and uncertainty

## What should you ask yourself?

- Where in my life do I want more room to choose?
- What responsibilities or pressures have been taking up my attention lately?
- What did I want to move toward or away from in the dream?
- When do I feel most in control of my direction?
- Which part of the dream felt most like something I am experiencing now?

## How do you write this dream down?

Next time this dream appears, write down where you were flying, how you moved, and what you could see below. Record the feeling at the start and end, then note any current choices, pressures, or changes that came to mind.
