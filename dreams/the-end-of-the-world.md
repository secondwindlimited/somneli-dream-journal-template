# What does it mean to dream about the end of the world?

Dreams about the end of the world can feel intense, but they do not predict or warn of real events. People often connect them with major change, pressure, uncertainty, or feeling powerless. These associations are starting points, not fixed meanings.

## What are dreams about the end of the world often connected with?

- Major transition
- Feeling overloaded
- Loss of control
- Wider uncertainty

## What should you ask yourself?

- What has felt uncertain or out of my control lately?
- Which changes in my life feel like endings, even if they are also beginnings?
- Who was present in the dream, and what is my relationship with them like now?
- What did I try to protect, leave behind, or carry with me?
- Did the dream's feeling resemble anything I have experienced while awake?

## How do you write this dream down?

Next time this dream appears, write down the first scene you remember, what changed or ended, who was there, and what you did. Record the strongest feeling before interpreting it. Add any recent pressures or transitions, then note which associations fit and which do not.
