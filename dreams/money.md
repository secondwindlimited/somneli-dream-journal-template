# What does it mean to dream about money?

Money dreams are often linked with security, choice, self-worth, or fairness, especially during changes in work, spending, or relationships. These are starting points, not fixed meanings. The amount, source, and feeling in the dream can offer more personal clues.

## What are dreams about money often connected with?

- Security and pressure
- Worth and recognition
- Control and scarcity
- Fairness and obligation

## What should you ask yourself?

- What money-related pressure or change is present in my life right now?
- What did I want the money to help me do in the dream?
- Did I feel in control of the money, and where do I feel that way when awake?
- What does having enough mean to me at this point in my life?
- Did the dream bring up fairness or obligation with someone close to me?

## How do you write this dream down?

Write down the amount, where the money came from, what happened to it, and who else was there. Name the strongest feeling and connect it with a current work, spending, or relationship concern, without forcing a single explanation.
