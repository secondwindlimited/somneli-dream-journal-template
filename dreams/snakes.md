# What does it mean to dream about snakes?

A snake in a dream has no fixed meaning. People commonly connect it with threat, change, guarded trust, or something they are trying not to face. Your own associations, the snake’s actions, and the dream’s mood may point elsewhere.

## What are dreams about snakes often connected with?

- Threat and vigilance
- Change and renewal
- Trust and boundaries
- Avoided concerns

## What should you ask yourself?

- What in my life currently feels uncertain or hard to control?
- Where might I need clearer boundaries or communication?
- What change am I adjusting to, if any?
- What did the snake do, and how did I respond?
- What personal memories or associations do snakes bring up for me?

## How do you write this dream down?

Next time this dream appears, write down where you were, what the snake did, and how you felt at each point. Note any waking-life pressures or changes from that day, then record your own associations without treating them as a fixed explanation.
