# What does it mean to dream about a partner cheating?

A dream about a partner cheating can stir jealousy, fear, or anger. It is not evidence that your partner is cheating or will cheat. People often connect it with insecurity, distance, past hurt, or a need for reassurance, though those associations may not fit.

## What are dreams about a partner cheating often connected with?

- Trust and security
- Distance and change
- Self-worth and comparison
- Boundaries and communication

## What should you ask yourself?

- When have I felt most secure or unsettled in my relationship lately?
- What recent change or stress might be shaping this dream?
- What do I need more clarity or reassurance about?
- Where am I comparing myself with someone else?
- What boundary or conversation would help me feel heard?

## How do you write this dream down?

Write down what happened in the dream, who was present, and the moment that felt strongest. Name the feelings and any details that stood out. Then note what has been happening in your waking life, without treating the dream as evidence about your partner.
