# What does it mean to dream about water?

Water in dreams is often associated with emotion, change, uncertainty, or renewal, but these readings are not universal and may not fit you. Notice the water’s condition, your actions, and what was happening in your waking life.

## What are dreams about water often connected with?

- Emotional intensity
- Change and uncertainty
- Calm and renewal
- Control and boundaries

## What should you ask yourself?

- What has felt emotionally intense or hard to contain lately?
- Where in my life do I feel carried along by change?
- What would calm or clear space look like for me right now?
- Which boundaries or personal limits feel important to protect?
- What detail of the water stayed with me after waking?

## How do you write this dream down?

When this dream appears again, record the water’s color, movement, depth, and location. Note what you did, who else was there, and how you felt before and after waking. Add one waking-life situation that comes to mind, without forcing a connection.
