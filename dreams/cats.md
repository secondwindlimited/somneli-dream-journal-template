# What does it mean to dream about cats?

Cats in dreams are often associated with independence, comfort, caution, or feeling misunderstood. These are starting points, not fixed meanings. Your relationship with cats, the cat's behavior, and what is happening in your waking life may make the image feel different.

## What are dreams about cats often connected with?

- Independence and boundaries
- Comfort and companionship
- Alertness and uncertainty
- Care and responsibility

## What should you ask yourself?

- What was the cat doing, and what did I want to do in response?
- Where in my life do I want more space or closeness right now?
- Is there a responsibility I feel protective of, or tired by?
- What recent moment had a similar feeling, even if the details differed?
- What do I personally associate with cats, based on my experience?

## How do you write this dream down?

Next time this dream appears, record where the cat was, what it did, and how close it came. Note your strongest feeling, any waking-life situation it brings to mind, and your personal memories of cats. Leave room for the possibility that no association feels right.
