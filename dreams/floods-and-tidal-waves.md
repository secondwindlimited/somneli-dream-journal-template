# What does it mean to dream about floods and tidal waves?

Floods and tidal waves are often associated with overwhelm, powerful feelings, or change that seems hard to control. These are possible starting points, not fixed meanings. Your waking circumstances and the water’s behavior may offer more useful clues.

## What are dreams about floods and tidal waves often connected with?

- Feeling overwhelmed
- Strong emotions
- Change and uncertainty
- Boundaries under pressure

## What should you ask yourself?

- What has felt difficult to keep up with lately?
- Which feelings have I been holding back or making room for?
- What change currently feels outside my control?
- Where do I need more space or support?
- What helped me feel safer in the dream, if anything?

## How do you write this dream down?

If this dream returns, write down where the water appeared, how quickly it moved, and what you did. Name the strongest feeling and any waking-life pressure or change it brings to mind. Note what fits and what does not.
