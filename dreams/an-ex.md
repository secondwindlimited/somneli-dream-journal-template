# What does it mean to dream about an ex-partner?

Dreaming about an ex-partner can bring up memories, feelings, or current relationship concerns, but it does not prove you want them back or reveal what they feel. The details and your waking-life context offer a more personal place to begin reflecting.

## What are dreams about an ex-partner often connected with?

- Unfinished feelings
- Current relationship patterns
- A past version of you
- Seeking closure

## What should you ask yourself?

- What was happening in my life around the time this dream appeared?
- Which feeling stayed with me after I woke up?
- What detail of the relationship or breakup stood out?
- Does this dream resemble a current situation or concern?
- What do I want to carry forward, and what can I leave behind?

## How do you write this dream down?

Write down the scene, what your ex said or did, and the feeling you woke with. Note any recent reminders or changes in your life. Record what felt familiar, what differed from reality, and what personal association comes to mind without treating it as a conclusion.
