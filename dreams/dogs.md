# What does it mean to dream about dogs?

Dreams about dogs are often associated with companionship, trust, protection, responsibility, or tension. These are starting points, not fixed meanings, and may not fit you. Notice what the dog did, how familiar it felt, and what was happening in your waking life.

## What are dreams about dogs often connected with?

- Companionship and trust
- Protection and boundaries
- Care and responsibility
- Tension and mistrust

## What should you ask yourself?

- What did the dog’s behavior remind me of in my current life?
- Where do I feel supported, or where would I like more support?
- Am I carrying a responsibility that feels welcome or burdensome?
- What boundary or conversation has been on my mind lately?
- How do my real-life experiences with dogs shape this image for me?

## How do you write this dream down?

Next time, write down what the dog looked like, what it did, and how you felt at each moment. Note any people, places, or recent responsibilities in the dream, then connect them with current circumstances without forcing a single meaning.
