# What does it mean to dream about someone dying?

Dreams about someone dying can feel vivid, but they do not predict a death or provide evidence about anyone’s real-life health, feelings, or behaviour. People often connect them with change, worry, distance, or grief. These associations are possibilities, not a universal dictionary.

## What are dreams about someone dying often connected with?

- Change and transition
- Worry about someone
- Grief and absence
- Helplessness and responsibility

## What should you ask yourself?

- What changes or endings have been on my mind lately?
- Who have I felt worried about, distant from, or unable to help?
- What did I feel during the dream, and where have I felt that while awake?
- What detail about the person or setting stayed with me, and why might it matter to me?
- What support or conversation would feel useful in my current situation?

## How do you write this dream down?

Write who died, what happened before and after, and how close or distant you felt from them. Record the strongest emotion and any recent changes, worries, losses, or reminders. Note what feels relevant to you, and what does not.
