# What does it mean to dream about being chased?

Being chased in a dream is often associated with pressure, avoidance, or feeling unsafe, but these readings may not fit you. Notice who or what is pursuing you, where you are, and whether you run, hide, turn around, or get help.

## What are dreams about being chased often connected with?

- Pressure and demands
- An avoided concern
- Crowded boundaries
- Feeling outmatched

## What should you ask yourself?

- What has felt difficult to avoid or put off lately?
- Where in my life do I feel pressured to keep moving?
- What did I feel toward the pursuer, if I recognized them?
- What helped me feel safer or more in control in the dream?
- What boundary or support might I need right now?

## How do you write this dream down?

Next time this dream appears, write down who or what was chasing you, where it happened, and what you did. Record the strongest feeling and any recent waking-life pressures or changes. Note your own associations without treating them as a fixed explanation.
