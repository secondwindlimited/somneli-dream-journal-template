# What does it mean to dream about being attacked?

Dreams about being attacked are often associated with pressure, conflict, vulnerability, or feeling unable to protect your boundaries. These are possible starting points, not fixed meanings. The dream does not predict an attack; consider what was happening in your life and how the scene felt.

## What are dreams about being attacked often connected with?

- Conflict or pressure
- Personal boundaries
- Loss of control
- Unacknowledged feelings

## What should you ask yourself?

- What felt most threatening in the scene, and why?
- Where have I felt pressured or unable to set a boundary lately?
- What did I do during the attack, and how did that feel?
- Does the attacker remind me of a real conflict, or not?
- What support or space would help me feel safer now?

## How do you write this dream down?

Next time, write down where the attack happened, who or what was involved, what happened just before it, and how you responded. Record the strongest feeling and any recent waking-life stress that comes to mind. Leave room for the dream to have no clear connection.
