# What does it mean to dream about elevators?

Elevator dreams are often read through movement, control, and destination, but there is no fixed meaning. They may appear during change, pressure, or uncertainty. Your feelings and waking circumstances matter more than any universal symbol.

## What are dreams about elevators often connected with?

- Change and transition
- Control and direction
- Progress and pressure
- Feeling stuck

## What should you ask yourself?

- What change or transition is taking up space in my life right now?
- Where do I feel able to choose my direction, and where do I feel carried along?
- What destination or floor stood out, and what does it bring to mind for me?
- What expectations about progress am I responding to lately?
- What in my life feels delayed, uncertain, or ready to move?

## How do you write this dream down?

If this dream returns, write down the elevator's direction, speed, condition, floors, and other people present. Record what you felt before, during, and after the ride. Then note any current changes, delays, or decisions that seem relevant, without forcing a connection.
