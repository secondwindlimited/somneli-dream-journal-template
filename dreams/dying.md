# What does it mean to dream about your own death?

Dreams of your own death can feel vivid, but they are not evidence about your real-life safety or future events. People often connect them with change, stress, endings, or uncertainty. These are possible associations, not fixed meanings, and they may not fit you.

## What are dreams about your own death often connected with?

- Change or transition
- Feeling powerless
- A changing identity
- Stress or mortality

## What should you ask yourself?

- What has felt like it is ending or changing in my life?
- Where do I feel I have little control right now?
- What role or expectation feels different from before?
- What was on my mind in the days before this dream?
- Which feeling stayed with me after I woke up?

## How do you write this dream down?

Next time, write down what happened just before and after your death in the dream, who was there, and the strongest feeling. Note any recent changes, pressures, losses, or sleep disruptions, then record your own associations without treating them as predictions.
